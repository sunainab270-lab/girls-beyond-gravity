# Marketing Strategy Draft

Newsletter: opportunity of the week, deadlines, aerospace news, physics concept, featured career, role model, project, and event.

Ambassador program: future applications for students who start clubs, translate resources, verify regional opportunities, recruit mentors, and report local impact.
