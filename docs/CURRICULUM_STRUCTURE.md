# Curriculum Structure

Grade 9: Foundations
- Introduction to Physics
- Motion
- Forces
- Energy
- Momentum
- Introduction to Flight and Space

Grade 10: Core Physics
- Advanced Motion
- Dynamics
- Waves and Sound
- Electricity and Circuits
- Thermal Physics
- Physics of Aircraft

Grade 11: Advanced Physics
- Advanced Mechanics
- Gravitation and Orbits
- Fluid Mechanics
- Electricity and Magnetism
- Thermodynamics
- Oscillations and Waves
- Introduction to Propulsion

Grade 12: Aerospace and College Preparation
- Orbital Mechanics
- Aerodynamics
- Rocket Propulsion
- Aircraft Performance
- Spacecraft Systems
- Modern and Space Physics
- Engineering Design Project

Every unit should support diagnostics, objectives, vocabulary, lessons, approved video placeholders, examples, simulations, practice, aerospace applications, safe activities, review, tests, feedback, and challenge extensions.
