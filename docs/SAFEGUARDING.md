# Safeguarding Policy Draft

Draft only. Requires qualified safeguarding and legal review before launch.

Principles:

- Minimize personal data collection.
- Do not display exact student locations publicly.
- No public personal contact details for minors.
- Group tutoring and mentoring by default.
- No unsupervised private adult-to-minor messaging.
- Guardian consent where required.
- Verified tutor and mentor workflows.
- Reporting, blocking, moderation, and audit logs.
- Clear escalation pathways for concerns.
- Codes of conduct for tutors, mentors, students, educators, and volunteers.
