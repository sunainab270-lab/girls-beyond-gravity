# AI Tutor Safety

The MVP includes a mock AI tutor interface only.

Required behavior:

- Provide progressive help: small hint, stronger hint, next step, similar worked example, then full explanation when appropriate.
- Do not provide direct answer assistance during active graded assessments.
- Cite relevant internal lessons in future real integrations.
- Clearly state uncertainty.
- Store minimal chat data.
- Allow clearing chat history.
- Display that the tutor is not a human teacher and may be inaccurate.

Production integration needs model safety review, logging policy, abuse monitoring, and privacy review.
