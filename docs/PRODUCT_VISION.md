# Product Vision

Beyond Gravity is a free platform that helps aspiring aerospace students:

1. Master AP Physics through high-quality educational content.
2. Discover aerospace scholarships, undergraduate programs, internships, research opportunities, summer programs, and competitions around the world.

The visitor should understand the product in one sentence:

Beyond Gravity helps aspiring aerospace students master AP Physics and discover scholarships, internships, undergraduate programs, and other opportunities.

Mentorship, community, clubs, news, portfolios, country guides, company directories, educator dashboards, ambassadors, and standalone career exploration are inactive future features.
