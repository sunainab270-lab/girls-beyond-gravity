# Content Guidelines

Do not publish invented testimonials, impact statistics, partner logos, endorsements, salary claims, employment claims, visa advice, or legal eligibility claims.

Do not provide instructions for dangerous rocket motors, propellant production, explosive materials, unsafe pressure systems, weapon construction, or unsupervised hazardous experiments.

Physical activities must include safety requirements, adult-supervision guidance where appropriate, age suitability, protective equipment, and safer simulation alternatives.

News must use original short summaries and links to official sources. Do not republish full copyrighted articles.
